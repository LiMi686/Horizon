## [GPs Conditioned on Almost Anything via Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21041) ⭐️ 9.0/10

This paper establishes an explicit equivalence between Gaussian processes and linear diffusion models, enabling exact GP conditioning on arbitrary likelihoods such as nonlinear physics and natural language via a simple Monte Carlo approximation. This breakthrough extends Gaussian process inference beyond the traditional linear-Gaussian regime, allowing practitioners to incorporate complex, real-world knowledge—including outputs from large language models—as conditioning information without bespoke derivations. The method recovers standard GP conditioning exactly in the linear-Gaussian case and uses whitening to isolate non-Gaussian dynamics, minimizing Wasserstein-2 transport cost and eliminating numerical stiffness.

rss · arXiv - Data Science & Statistics · May 21, 04:00

**Background**: Gaussian processes are a powerful Bayesian tool for modeling functions, but exact inference is limited to conjugate (linear-Gaussian) settings. Diffusion models are a class of generative models that reverse a noising process. This work bridges the two, providing a general inference scheme for GPs conditioned on arbitrary likelihoods.
