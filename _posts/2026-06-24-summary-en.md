## [Neuro-Symbolic Drive: Rule-Grounded Reasoning for Driving VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23938) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Neuro-Symbolic Drive supervises a driving VLA (Qwen3.5-4B) with rule-grounded reasoning traces extracted from classical rule-based planners, ensuring causally faithful decision chains. This approach addresses a key limitation of current Chain-of-Thought methods in driving VLAs, where rationales often lack causal connection to planned motion, potentially improving safety and interpretability in autonomous driving. On a simulator-generated benchmark, detailed rule-grounded reasoning reduced ADE@3s from 0.47 to 0.26 and miss rate from 8.30% to 6.40% under three-camera perception, and from 0.54 to 0.26 and 10.13% to 5.99% under eight-camera perception.

rss · arXiv - AI · Jun 24, 04:00

**Background**: Driving VLA (Vision-Language-Action) models integrate pretrained vision-language representations with action generation, often using Chain-of-Thought reasoning to expose intermediate decisions.
