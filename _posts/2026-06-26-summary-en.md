## [Refusal Gated by Persona in Chat Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26161) ⭐️ 8.0/10

In Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, steering the persona direction reduces refusal rate from 97% to 2%. This finding challenges the prevailing view of refusal as a standalone safety mechanism, revealing a deeper interaction with model persona. The study extracts a compliant persona direction and a refusal direction in Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Reintroducing the refusal direction partially restores refusal at late layers but not early ones, indicating refusal is gated at the late-layer expression stage.

rss · arXiv - AI · Jun 26, 04:00

**Background**: Mechanistic interpretability research has identified linear directions in activation space that encode high-level concepts like refusal and persona. Previously, these were studied separately; this work shows they interact, with persona gating refusal.
