## [Forge: Guardrails Boost 8B Model Accuracy from 53% to 99%](https://github.com/antoinezambelli/forge) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Antoine Zambelli released Forge, an open-source reliability layer that uses domain-agnostic guardrails to boost a local 8B LLM's accuracy on multi-step agentic tasks from 53% to 99.3% without changing the model. This demonstrates that small local models, with proper guardrails, can rival frontier APIs like Claude Sonnet, reducing reliance on expensive cloud services and enabling cost-effective, private agentic systems. Forge's guardrail stack includes five toggleable layers: retry nudges, error recovery, step enforcement, rescue parsing, and context compaction; retry nudges alone account for 24-49 point accuracy drops when disabled. The project also introduces a ToolResolutionError exception to distinguish successful tool runs from empty results.

hackernews · zambelli · May 19, 12:23 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48192383)

**Background**: Agentic tasks involve multi-step workflows where an LLM calls tools and processes results. Small local models often suffer from compounding errors: 90% per-step accuracy yields only ~40% success over 5 steps. Guardrails are rule-based or AI-assisted mechanisms that enforce safe and reliable LLM behavior.
