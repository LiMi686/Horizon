Iroh uses QUIC connections identified by public keys (EndpointId), supports NAT traversal via relays and hole-punching, and now allows custom transports beyond the built-in IPv4, IPv6, and relay support.

**Background**: Traditional networking relies on IP addresses, which are temporary and change frequently, causing connection breaks. Iroh treats IP addresses as a low-level detail and instead uses cryptographic keys for peer identity, similar to how Tailscale works at the network layer but at the application layer. This allows apps to embed peer-to-peer connectivity without requiring users to have separate VPN accounts.
