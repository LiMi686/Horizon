This work bridges adversarial robustness and lattice theory, offering a novel theoretical framework that could lead to more efficient verification methods for neural networks, enhancing AI safety. The paper defines sound certification (no change inside interval) and complete certification (guaranteed change outside interval), and shows that complete certification optimization is solvable in polynomial oracle calls while sound certification is intractable.

This paper introduces lattice traversal as a new perspective to unify and extend these concepts.

## [CogniConsole: Externalizing Inference-Time Control for Reliable LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08774) ⭐️ 8.0/10

CogniConsole introduces a structured architectural abstraction that externalizes inference-time control for LLMs, combining programmatic coordination with bounded prompt-based reasoning. Experiments with controllability-oriented probes (N=489) show that increasing structural scaffolding systematically reduces output variance and failure rates under a fixed model architecture. This work challenges the prevailing view that LLM reliability is primarily a function of model capability, showing that inference-time control is a first-class factor. It opens new design directions for building more reliable LLM systems without relying solely on scaling, which is crucial for practical deployment in safety-critical applications. The study uses controllability-oriented probes in a multi-step interactive environment with 489 samples, comparing unstructured, partially scaffolded, and fully scaffolded control configurations. Results indicate that many failure modes, such as context drift and inconsistent constraint adherence, stem from under-specified control rather than insufficient capability.

rss · arXiv - AI · Jul 13, 04:00

**Background**: Large language models (LLMs) generate text by predicting tokens, and their behavior can be influenced by inference-time parameters like temperature and top-p. However, these parameters offer limited control over complex, multi-step tasks. CogniConsole proposes a formal abstraction that externalizes control into a structured interface, similar to how game consoles separate hardware from game cartridges, enabling more reliable and predictable LLM interactions.
