## [Elkjop fined €1.8M for forced consent in customer club](https://www.thatprivacyguy.com/blog/elkjop-forced-consent-fine/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) fined electronics retailer Elkjop NOK 20 million (approx. €1.8 million) for violating GDPR by requiring customers to consent to marketing as a condition of joining its customer club. This case demonstrates that GDPR's consent requirements are enforceable with significant penalties, even for long-standing practices, and sends a strong warning to companies that bundle consent with service access. The fine was issued five years after a privacy advocate first complained in 2021, and the company's own statement that 'in order to receive marketing/offers, it is a condition to be a member of the customer club' was key evidence of forced consent.

hackernews · speckx · Jun 18, 18:31 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48589501)

**Background**: Under GDPR, consent must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous; it cannot be a precondition for a service unless strictly necessary. Forced consent occurs when a user cannot access a service without agreeing to data processing that is not essential for that service. Elkjop's customer club required marketing consent for membership, which the DPA ruled invalid.

This demonstrates the real-world effectiveness of HPV vaccination over more than 15 years, offering strong evidence that the vaccine can virtually eliminate cervical cancer deaths in vaccinated populations. The study analyzed data from the UK HPV vaccination program, which started in 2008 for girls aged 12-13, and found that cervical cancer deaths among vaccinated women were extremely rare.
