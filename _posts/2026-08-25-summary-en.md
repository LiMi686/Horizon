## [KVBoost: Chunk-Level KV Cache Reuse for Efficient LLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21362) ⭐️ 8.0/10

KVBoost introduces a chunk-level KV cache reuse system that enables reuse of cached key-value tensors at arbitrary positions, not just contiguous prefixes. It uses a dual-hash keying scheme and deviation-guided recomputation to maintain accuracy, achieving a 4.49x reduction in time-to-first-token on Qwen2.5-3B. This work addresses a key limitation of existing prefix-caching systems, which only reuse KV caches for shared leading prefixes. By enabling chunk-level reuse at arbitrary positions, KVBoost can significantly reduce prefill latency for a wider range of workloads, improving the efficiency of LLM inference services. KVBoost employs two repair strategies: SelectiveRecompute and CacheBlendRecompute, to handle attention boundary errors. It also incorporates asymmetric KV quantization (int8/int4), adaptive chunk boundary splitting, and importance-weighted eviction under a fixed memory budget.

rss · arXiv - AI · Aug 25, 04:00

**Background**: Transformer-based LLMs recompute key-value (KV) tensors for each request, causing high prefill latency. Prefix caching reduces this by reusing KV caches for shared prefixes, but it fails when shared content appears at non-prefix positions. KVBoost extends this idea to chunk-level reuse, using a dual-hash scheme to identify reusable chunks and repair strategies to correct attention errors.
