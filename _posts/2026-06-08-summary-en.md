## [Generative Models Erode Human Learning via Market Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06572) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A new paper introduces 'value collapse,' a framework showing that generative models make it economically unviable to verify whether outputs reflect genuine human learning, leading to erosion of path-dependent knowledge accumulation. This reveals a structural risk of generative AI that persists even with better alignment, potentially undermining knowledge production in academia, law, content platforms, and software security. The paper formalizes value collapse using a costly-inspection framework and presents cross-domain evidence mapping four stages of verification erosion across academic publishing, legal practice, content platforms, and software security.

rss · arXiv - Machine Learning · Jun 8, 04:00

**Background**: Human Temporal Learning (HTL) refers to path-dependent knowledge accumulation through sustained engagement over time. Generative models can produce outputs that closely resemble HTL-intensive work, making it costly to distinguish human from AI contributions. When verification becomes uneconomical, evaluators reward outputs regardless of origin, and human producers compete on price against near-zero-cost AI outputs.
