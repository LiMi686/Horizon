## [First Lower Bound Beating d√T for Bandit Convex Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18652) ⭐️ 9.0/10

This paper proves an Ω̃(d^{5/4}√T) minimax expected regret lower bound for stochastic bandit convex optimization of 1-Lipschitz functions on the Euclidean ball, the first nontrivial lower bound that grows faster than d√T. This result establishes a fundamental separation between bandit convex optimization and linear bandits, showing the former is strictly harder, and will influence future algorithm design and lower bound techniques in online learning. The hard instance uses a class of convex functions in 2d dimensions involving a soft maximum of a tube and a squared distance function, forcing the learner to discover an unknown linear transformation to gain information.

rss · arXiv - Data Science & Statistics · Jul 22, 04:00

**Background**: Bandit convex optimization (BCO) is a framework where a learner repeatedly chooses actions and only observes the value of a convex loss function at that point, without gradient information. The goal is to minimize regret compared to the best fixed action. Prior work had established a d√T lower bound for linear bandits, but for general convex functions the best known lower bound was only d√T, leaving open whether BCO is harder.
