The study conducted 900 sessions across three commercial LLMs using three-turn escalating vulnerability vignettes with material, relational, and somatic status-proxy variants, coding responses with VCC/VCI binary indices.

**Background**: Large language models often face a trade-off between being helpful and avoiding harm, especially when users in vulnerable states request information that could reinforce maladaptive beliefs. The proposed design principle, Minimal Reattributive Sufficiency (MRS), aims to embed a single reattributive cue within a validating response to preserve a pathway toward autonomous reattribution without contesting the user's stated goal.
