## [Diffusion Models Achieve Near-Optimal Sample Complexity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30153) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A new paper proves that diffusion models require at most Õ(ε^{-k ∨ 2}) samples to achieve ε error in 1-Wasserstein distance when learning distributions supported on a union of low-dimensional subspaces, where k is the intrinsic dimension. This result provides a rigorous theoretical justification for the empirical success of diffusion models on high-dimensional data with low-dimensional structure, showing they can avoid the curse of dimensionality and adapt to intrinsic complexity. The bound depends only on the intrinsic dimension k, not the ambient dimension, and holds without smoothness, bounded-density, or log-concavity assumptions, covering a broad class of multi-modal distributions.

rss · arXiv - Data Science & Statistics · May 29, 04:00

**Background**: Diffusion models, also known as score-based generative models, learn to generate data by reversing a noise-adding process. Sample complexity measures how many training samples are needed to achieve a given error. Prior theoretical guarantees often suffered from the curse of dimensionality, requiring exponentially many samples in the ambient dimension.

## [CCT: Category-Theoretic Inductive Biases Cut Perplexity 12%](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28864) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Cognitive Categorical Transformer (CCT), a 306M-parameter architecture augmenting GPT-2 Small with category-theoretic and cognitive components, achieves 21.27 validation perplexity on WikiText-103, a 12% relative reduction over the baseline GPT-2 Small's 24.19 perplexity under matched training conditions. This work provides the first ablation-validated evidence that simplicial message passing, a topological deep learning technique, can improve language model perplexity at scale, opening a new direction for integrating structured inductive biases from category theory and cognitive science into neural language models. Ablation studies show that 84% of the perplexity improvement (2.45 out of 2.92 PPL) is attributable to the GT-Full simplicial message passing component. The paper also reports three negative results for consistency-style categorical priors (sheaf smoothing, adjunction round-trip, curvature regularization), supporting a structure/consistency distinction.

rss · arXiv - AI · May 29, 04:00

**Background**: Inductive biases are assumptions that guide a learning algorithm toward certain patterns. Category theory provides a high-level mathematical framework for composing structures, and simplicial message passing extends graph neural networks to higher-order topological structures like simplicial complexes. This work combines these ideas to inject structured priors into a transformer-based language model.

## [Orthogonal Concept Erasure for Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28902) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers propose Orthogonal Concept Erasure (OCE), which replaces additive parameter updates with multiplicative orthogonal transformations to erase concepts in diffusion models without interfering with overall generative capacity. This work addresses a fundamental limitation of editing-based concept erasure methods, enabling precise removal of unsafe content while preserving model performance, which is crucial for safe AI deployment. OCE applies layer-wise orthogonal transformations derived from a closed-form solution, and introduces subspace-level objectives for multi-concept erasure, achieving erasure of up to 100 concepts in 4.3 seconds.

**Background**: Concept erasure aims to modify diffusion models to prevent generation of specific unsafe content (e.g., NSFW images) without retraining. Editing-based methods are efficient but often degrade overall generation quality due to additive parameter updates that entangle direction, magnitude, and angular geometry of neurons.
