## [OmniMem Compresses KV Cache for Streaming Audio-Visual LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07577) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OmniMem introduces a perturbation-aware memory compression framework for streaming audio-visual LLMs, featuring modality-aware memory allocation and perturbation-aware selection to compress KV caches. It achieves 2-4% absolute accuracy improvement over training-free baselines under the same memory budget, with an additional 1-2% gain after fine-tuning. Long-form video understanding is a critical bottleneck for audio-visual LLMs due to linearly growing video tokens and KV caches. OmniMem's efficient compression enables practical streaming inference on long videos, potentially advancing applications like real-time video analysis and multimodal dialogue systems. OmniMem separately manages visual and audio contexts to address token imbalance, and uses perturbation-aware selection to preserve informative, non-redundant KV states. It also explores budget-aware fine-tuning to further consolidate useful information into retained memory.

rss · arXiv - AI · Jun 9, 04:00

**Background**: Audio-visual large language models (LLMs) combine visual and audio inputs for tasks like video understanding. During inference, they store key-value (KV) caches from previous tokens to avoid recomputation, but these caches grow linearly with video length, causing memory bottlenecks. Existing compression methods treat all tokens uniformly, ignoring modality-specific characteristics.

Tokamaks are doughnut-shaped devices that use magnetic fields to confine plasma for nuclear fusion; DIII-D is a leading tokamak in the US. Plasma control involves regulating multiple profiles (e.g., density, temperature) over long horizons, a complex control problem.
